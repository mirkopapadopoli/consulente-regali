# Surface brief — pagina consulente (/) e risultato condiviso (/r/<id>)

Scope: home (hero, ticker, come funziona, occasioni, invito), search states (ho capito, timeline, gift tiles, light mode, messages) and the shared result page. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience/job: people who don't know what to give; many on a phone inside Instagram, many planning on desktop. Task: write one sentence, read three gifts with their reason, open Amazon.

Constraints: Italian copy, affiliate disclosure visible, fully centered layout, responsive 390px → wide desktop, light mode intentional.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A brief-pinned world — Mirko supplied The Verge's 2024 system (getdesign "theverge", kept in docs/context/theverge-DESIGN.md) after rejecting "Officina". The page is a tech-magazine front page about one person's gift, refusing the generic white gift-finder.

OWN-WORLD: Canvas #131313, mint #3cffd0 and ultraviolet #5200ff hazard accents, yellow #ffd23f third tile; Anton display, Hanken Grotesk UI, JetBrains Mono UPPERCASE labels, Newsreader for the "perché"; 20–40px radii, 1px rules, no shadows or gradients.

STORY: Visitor sees "cosaregalo?" and the promise "Tre regali su misura, in pochi secondi.", writes or picks an occasion, watches the timeline of three searches, reads three color-block gifts and opens Amazon or shares.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky bar (wordmark appears after scroll, links, mint CTA); centered whisper, giant wordmark with mint "?", one-line headline on desktop, mono label, bordered textarea, mint pill, example pills.

FORM: User-pinned template (no concept-seed roll; the earlier seed f4c2af3a belonged to the rejected Officina world).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
